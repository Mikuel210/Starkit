**When encountering an obstacle, pause to analyze root causes instead of immediately trying random fixes.**
**Work on focused, uninterrupted sessions with no multitasking.**
**Do not cut on drying time**
**Always make test pieces before printing full parts**
**Test pieces don't always behave like full parts. Oversize holes and use another locking mechanism.**
**Don't go below the width of auto brims.**
**Do not force fits.**

Printing orientation affects the fit massively
A longer hole means more friction

- Carbon rod tolerance
	- Horizontal
		- 5mm: 0.2 + chamfer (could be tighter)
	- Vertical
		- 5mm: 0.15 (really tight fit), 0.2 (tight still)
		- 3mm: 0.42 on leg (perfect fit, do not touch), 0.25 on test pieces
- Bolt tolerance: 0.2

Max. spring length: >= 14cm
